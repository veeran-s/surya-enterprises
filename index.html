About Suriya Enterprises

Suriya Enterprises is a digital manufacturing solutions company led by D.K. Durairaj, providing complete sales and services in advanced industrial and engineering solutions.

The company specializes in cattle and poultry feed mill machinery, masala machineries and spares, along with modern technologies like 3D scanning, 3D reverse engineering, 3D and 2D inspection, and 3D printing.

In addition to this, Suriya Enterprises offers expertise in jigs and fixtures, sheet metal works, press tools, mould dies, pressure die casting, gravity die casting, patterns, special machineries, and various allied engineering projects.

With a strong focus on quality, precision, and customer satisfaction, the company delivers reliable and efficient solutions for a wide range of industrial needs.

Based in RK Puram, Ganapathy, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu
