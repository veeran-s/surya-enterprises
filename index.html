About Suriya Enterprises

Suriya Enterprises is a leading manufacturer specializing in high-quality machine fixtures, jigs, press tools, and mold dies. With a strong focus on innovation and precision engineering, we also design and develop advanced animal feed plant machineries and masala processing machines. Our expertise extends to 3D scanning, reverse engineering, 3D printing, and computer-aided design (CAD), enabling us to deliver customized and efficient solutions for diverse industrial needs. Backed by a team of experienced designers and skilled engineers, we are committed to providing reliable, cost-effective, and high-performance equipment for industries such as feed mills, automobiles, textiles, and allied sectors.

Based in RK Puram, Ganapathy, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu
