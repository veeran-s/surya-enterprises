Mr. D.K. Durairaj

Managing Director, Suriya Enterprises

About founder

Mr. D.K. Durairaj , Managing Director of SURIYA ENTERPRISES, heads the management with a strong foundation in tool engineering and precision manufacturing.

He is a proud 1980 batch alumnus of the Institute of Tool Engineering and currently serves as the Regional Joint Secretary – Coimbatore in the DTEAA Alumni Association.

With nearly 44 years of experience in this industry, he has successfully led numerous projects, building a proven record of results and long-term customer relationships.

His leadership, technical knowledge, and dedication to precision engineering continue to strengthen the company’s growth, quality standards, and customer satisfaction.

Vision and Mission

At SURIYA ENTERPRISES , our mission and vision are to deliver high-quality precision engineering solutions with reliability, innovation, and timely service while continuously improving technology, skilled workmanship, and customer satisfaction. We strive to become a trusted leader in manufacturing, tooling, automation, and special engineering solutions by maintaining international quality standards and achieving excellence through zero-defect production.

About Firm

Established in the year 2000, SURIYA ENTERPRISES is a certified firm manufacturing precision auto components for automobiles, valve and gear box components, jigs and fixtures, and special tools for various industrial applications.

Company History

The company has been promoted by a group of technocrats having long experience in a similar field. Since its incorporation, the company has maintained its group philosophy built on four strong pillars: quality, reliability, service, and continuous improvement. The workforce is drawn from the industrial community in and around Coimbatore, a prominent industrial city in India.

Present Activity

Our present activities cover a wide range of precision engineering, tooling and turnkey project solutions, including:

  • Computer Aided Design (AutoCAD, Creo, SolidWorks, CATIA & Design X)
  • Jigs & fixtures (HMC, VMC, CNC turning, welding & hydraulic fixtures)
  • Clamping systems with draw bar operation
  • Sheet metal components
  • Press tools and mould dies
  • Pressure and gravity die casting
  • Patterns & core boxes (wood, aluminium & steel)
  • Reverse gauges and special gauges
  • Back spot face tools
  • Special machineries and allied tooling projects
  • Cattle & poultry feed mills
  • Masala machineries & spares
  • Auto packing machines
  • 3D scanning, reverse engineering & 3D printing
  • Special features and customized engineering solutions
