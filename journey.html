Our Journey

A brief timeline of how Suriya Enterprises grew into a trusted partner for feed plants and engineering solutions.

1998 – Foundation

Suriya Enterprises started operations with a focus on precision machining and engineering services.

2005 – First Feed Plant Project

Successfully executed our first complete animal feed plant installation.

2015 – Expansion in Design & 3D

Adopted advanced 3D modelling, scanning and reverse engineering tools.

2024 – Digital Manufacturing Focus

Scaled to support mash and pellet plants from 2 TPH to 60 TPH along with modern engineering solutions.
